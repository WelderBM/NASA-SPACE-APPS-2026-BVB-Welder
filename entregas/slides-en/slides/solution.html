Gap: Schöning et al. (2023), Smart Agricultural Technology: almost all rotation tools optimize only profit, and all ignore farms of a few hundred m².

Here is the position. A peer-reviewed review of crop rotation tools found that almost all of them optimize only profit, and all of them ignore farms of a few hundred square meters. Ours lets the small farmer choose what to optimize: the soil, the risk, food at home, or pasture. Income is the one thing we don’t serve, and the tool says so. It starts from the harvest the farmer just made. Same harvest, same place: choose low risk and it says cowpea is quick, out in 70 days.
