HOW IT WORKS

Three numbers, not one score

Risk

Can I plant this here, now?

NASA POWER rain, soil moisture at three depths, and a year factor: the last 3 months against normal

Fit

Does it serve what I want?

Crop traits weighted by the farmer’s priority: nitrogen fixation, residue, drought tolerance, cycle, official planting window

Confidence

How much of this is measured?

The share of criteria we could evaluate. Every gap is listed in the answer, never filled with a guess.

Same code, opposite answers

Maize, 500 mm need. Cantá, April: 686 mm expected, risk low. Rio Verde, September: 386 mm, risk high. No location-specific code.

The official window counts

ZARC window for cowpea in Roraima: June to August. Low-risk fit: 53% in April, 87% in July. No window loaded: a declared gap, never a zero.

Honesty is tested

Our first engine ranked brachiaria first on 27% of its criteria. Now a gap left unsaid fails the test suite. No AI calculates or picks the crop.

Layers: NASA observation by coordinate · global crop traits · local crops and windows · farmer priority · thin channel adapter

Each option comes back with three separate numbers. Risk comes from NASA data for that coordinate and month: rain, soil moisture at three depths, and how this year is going. Fit comes from the crop’s traits weighted by what the farmer chose, and now also the official ZARC planting window: for cowpea in Roraima, the low-risk fit goes from 53 percent in April to 87 in July. Confidence says how much we could measure. The proof the core is generic: same maize, same code, low water risk in Cantá and high in Rio Verde, Goiás. And honesty is not a promise, it is a test: our first engine ranked the crop we knew least about first, so now any gap the answer does not say out loud breaks the test suite.
