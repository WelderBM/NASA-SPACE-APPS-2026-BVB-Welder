This decision is not invented by us. Right now, Roraima’s extension agency is telling family farmers to choose crops better adapted to the dry period. That is exactly our product, delivered today by human extension agents, and it reaches only whoever an agent reaches. In 2017, 87 percent of the state’s farm establishments had no technical assistance. In our own small survey, most families already plant something right after harvest, and drought is their main loss. And the cost is not only the lost crop: farmers told us people were afraid to invest at all.
