HOW IT WORKS

Three numbers, not one score

Risk

Can I plant this here, now?

NASA POWER rain (PRECTOTCORR), root-zone soil moisture (GWETROOT), and a year factor: the last 3 months vs normal

Fit

Does it serve what I want?

Crop traits weighted by the farmer’s priority: nitrogen fixation, residue, drought tolerance, cycle length

Confidence

How much of this is measured?

The share of criteria we could actually evaluate. What we don’t know is listed in the answer.

Same code, opposite answers

Maize, same 500 mm need. Cantá, RR, April: 621 mm expected this year, water risk low. Rio Verde, GO, September: 331 mm, water risk high. No line of location-specific code.

Why we split the score

Our first engine ranked brachiaria first using 27% of its criteria. Less data scored higher. The engine runs in the browser. AI understands and words, but never calculates, picks the crop or adds a number.

Layers: NASA observation by coordinate · global crop traits · local crop list · farmer priority · thin channel adapter

Each option comes back with three separate numbers. Risk comes from NASA data for that coordinate and that month, adjusted by how this year is going. Fit comes from the crop’s traits weighted by what the farmer chose. Confidence says how much of the answer we could measure. We split them because our first engine had a bug: the crop we knew least about ranked first. The proof that the core is generic: same maize, same code, low water risk in Cantá and high in Rio Verde, Goiás, where the seasons are reversed.
