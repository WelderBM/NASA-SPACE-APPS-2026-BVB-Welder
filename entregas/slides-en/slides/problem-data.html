All four numbers are ours, measured in NASA POWER for Cantá. June to September rain was 62 percent below the 1981 to 2025 average, the lowest since 1981. The longest dry spell inside the rainy season was 24 days, against 9. The soil entered the dry season at its yearly low, five months early. And El Niño is no rule: in El Niño years of our series, June to September rain ranged from 48 percent below to 17 percent above average. So we found no basis to say 2027 repeats 2026, so the tool doesn’t predict. It observes.
