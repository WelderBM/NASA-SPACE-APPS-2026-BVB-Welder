Asked about rain (Cantá, April): “I can’t forecast rain, I don’t have that. What I know is that April usually brings average rain, the longest dry spell of the month is usually 8 days, and the soil is at its average.”

Sources: Embrapa (Plantio Certo, RAImundo) · project repository, docs 10–18

We don’t compete with the official zoning or with Embrapa’s assistant. Plantio Certo answers when to plant a crop you already chose. We answer what should follow the harvest you just made. Two lines are our disadvantages, and we say them: we have no rain forecast, and we cover 14 crops, 4 with good data, against their 43. When a farmer asks about rain, the tool says it can’t forecast, and tells what it does know: what the month usually brings and how the soil is now. The first official ZARC window, cowpea in Roraima, is already in the engine and changes the answer; the rest is data to load.
