NASA POWER, Cantá (2.61, −60.60), monthly series 1981–2026, read Nov 14–15, 2026 (docs/04). Engine climatology: 812 mm, so −59%

All four numbers are ours, measured in NASA POWER for Cantá. June to September rain was 62 percent below the mean of the 1981 to 2026 series, the lowest since 1981. If someone says the mean includes 2026 itself: without 2026 the mean rises to about 871 millimeters and the drop is still 62 percent. And the engine uses a shorter climatology, 812 millimeters, which gives minus 59. The longest dry spell inside the rainy season was 24 days, against 9; that comes from the daily series, documented in docs/04, not stored in the repository. The soil entered the dry season at its yearly low, five months early. And El Niño is no rule: in the El Niño years before 2026, June to September rain ranged from 48 percent below to 17 percent above average; with 2026 the range reaches minus 62. So we found no basis to say 2027 repeats 2026, so the tool doesn’t predict. It observes.
