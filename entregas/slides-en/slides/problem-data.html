All four numbers are ours, measured in NASA POWER for Cantá. June to September rain was 62 percent below normal, the worst in 45 years. The longest dry spell inside the rainy season was 24 days, against 9. The soil entered the dry season at its yearly low, five months early. What we did not find is any basis to say 2027 repeats 2026, so the tool doesn’t predict. It observes.
